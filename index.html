About Ontee

Ontee Auto Parts Co., Ltd.

Ontee Auto Parts Co., Ltd. is an exporter, supplier and manufacturer of automotive parts. Our main export and supply products include shock absorbers and radiators for customers in the USA, Europe, Japan and Africa.

Our current export product categories also include brake system parts, suspension parts, hoods, doors, fenders and tailgates.
